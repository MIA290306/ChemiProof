Each cell = 10 volume ratios x 3 measurement rounds. Counts match the publication.

**Imbalance (a confounder, not corrected):** walnut has a single brand; Earl Grey appears only with rapeseed and corn; group sizes are 60/60/90/90/30. Brand and adulterant are partly entangled, so per-adulterant results also reflect brand mix.

## 4. Measurement format (measured)

- ASCII `.dat`, one EEM per file: **35 excitation** channels (300-640 nm; descending in the file, ascending after parsing) x **270 emission** channels (297.417-802.484 nm).
- One identical axis fingerprint across all 330 files; header `Normalized by 1.00` everywhere; intensity unit Counts/uA.
- Instrument settings are described by the authors and were not verified by us. All spectra come from **one instrument and one laboratory**.

## 5. Labels

- Folder ratio `a:b` is read as **olive : adulterant, parts by volume** (ADR-008), so `olive_fraction = a / 11`, values 1/11 ... 10/11 = 0.0909 ... 0.9091. No pure-olive (1.0) or pure-adulterant (0.0) sample exists.
- Evidence for the direction: the Zenodo description plus a spectral check (chlorophyll-band intensity is higher at the high-olive end in 11/11 brand x adulterant pairs; Spearman rho +0.82 to +1.00 in 8 pairs; weak for OliVera/corn +0.31, OliVera/peanut +0.39, OliVera/soybean -0.04). The paper's Methods text was not retrievable, so the direction rests on documentary + spectral evidence, not on an explicit statement by the authors.
- The adulterant fraction is `1 - olive_fraction` for these **binary** mixtures only.
- Chinese folder names were mapped to brand/adulterant by the agent and cross-checked against the published counts, not confirmed by the authors.

## 7. How ChemiProof uses the data

- Preprocessing `author_v1` (ADR-011; per spectrum, nothing fitted): subtract global minimum; Savitzky-Golay window 5 / order 2 along emission only (as in the authors' released code; the paper text says both axes); divide by global maximum; flatten to 9,450 features.
- Primary benchmark: five leave-one-adulterant-out (LOAO) folds; the held-out adulterant's labels are used only for scoring (ADR-013/014/017).
- The screening model FINAL-PLS-v1 is trained on all 330 spectra (ADR-018), so **no independent test set remains**.

## 8. Limitations for any reuse

- Laboratory binary mixtures of five adulterants, three EVOO brands, one instrument: nothing here represents commercial products, other adulterants, multi-adulterant blends, other instruments or other laboratories.
- Ten ratio levels, all between 9.1 % and 90.9 % olive oil.
- Measurement rounds may not be independent replicates (unproven).
- Integrity against the published archive unverified (section 2).
